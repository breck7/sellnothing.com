UPDATE: NEW PLAN!

Problem is true venture investors never stop looking for next thing, and their winners, while awesome, become less interesting (because 1 to N is less interesting to people obsessed with 0 to 1).

Lesson is: if you are an early stage investor, sell 10% of your remaining shares every single year (and no more).

You can eternally search for and fund new things but will maintain significant stakes in your winners:

  • after 10 years you'll still hold 35%!
  • even after 30 years you will still have 4% left of your stake!
